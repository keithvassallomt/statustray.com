# Appearance

The **Appearance** tab controls how tray icons look and which part of the top bar holds the tray.

![Appearance tab](/appearance-tab.png)

## Icons

### Icon style

Choose how tray icons are drawn:

- **Symbolic (monochrome)** (default): icons are converted to monochrome to match your system theme, light or dark. This is ideal for a consistent look.
- **Original (colored)**: icons keep their original colours, which can make them more visually distinct but may not blend as well with your system theme.

Here's an example of how the two styles look, with monochrome icons on the top and coloured icons on the bottom:

![Icon style comparison](/styles.png)

If an app's icon doesn't look right in the symbolic style, you can fine-tune it from the Apps tab. See [Icon Effects](/apps#icon-effects).

### Size

Sets the size of tray icons in the top bar. Sizes range from 14px (smallest) to 20px (largest), and the default of 16px is marked on the slider. The overflow button resizes to match.

### Padding between icons

Sets the gap between neighbouring tray icons. The value is the total gap, in pixels, between two icons, and ranges from 0px (icons sit flush together) to 20px. The default is 4px.

## Placement

### Panel position

Choose which part of the top bar holds the tray:

- **Left**: alongside the Activities button.
- **Centre**: alongside the clock.
- **Right** (default): in the status area on the right of the top bar.

In the left and centre positions, the tray is placed after whatever is already there, so the Activities button and the clock keep their places. If you've enabled [overflow](/behaviour#overflow), the overflow button moves with the tray.

::: info
Status Tray only lets you choose the section of the top bar. If you want to control exactly where the tray sits relative to other extensions' icons, use a panel-management extension.
:::
